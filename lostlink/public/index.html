Lost something? Found something?
Scan the tag.

Register your belongings once. School staff give you a QR tag. If your item goes missing, whoever finds it can scan the tag and reach you through the school.

Register an itemScan a QR tagCheck my item
1Register. Fill in the item, your name, and your student number.
2Get a tag. Staff check it and hand you a paper QR to stick on the item.
3Get it back. A finder scans the QR, sees whose it is, and reports where they found it.

Your contact number is never shown to the public. Only school staff can see it.
